3Ps is my master thesis project. This more academic work has been the occasion to explore the perception and opportunities offered by 3D printing technology as a production technique. The different items have been created after hearing the different participants of the project and explore some of the challenges brought by the technology (parametric modelling, printing time, design sturdiness, etc.).
