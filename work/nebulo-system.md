Nebulo System is a Swiss start-up that developed an alarm system which, instead of a sound, releases a thick fog preventing the burglar from entering the house. During the Innovation by Design challenge I worked with Fernando Riviero to develop a product around the knight figure that would fit in its environment with a strong identity. The materials we chose (ceramic and wood) give this product a warmer feeling.
