<h1 class="heading-sage">About me</h1>

Hi! My name is Salomé, and I am a 27-year-old French industrial designer. After completing my bachelor's at ECAL in Lausanne, Switzerland, I moved to Kolding in Denmark to pursue a master's in IT product design at SDU, from which I graduated a couple of years ago. I have now been living and working in Copenhagen for three years and enjoy the culturally rich and dynamic city that inspires me in so many ways.

I am a curious person who thrives on learning from new environments. I believe an open mindset is essential for good product development, as it fuels creativity and enables critical thinking. Additionally, I trust that working in interdisciplinary teams and engaging in collaborative practices are the best ways to gain valuable insights and develop relevant and innovative designs.

I am always open to new opportunities to apply my skills in an environment where I can continue to grow and learn.
